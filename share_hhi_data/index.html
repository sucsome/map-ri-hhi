About this map

This explorer maps the Racial HHI for every census block in Rhode Island (2020). The HHI (Herfindahl–Hirschman Index, scaled 0–10,000) measures how concentrated a block's population is across racial groups: a low value means the block is racially diverse, while a high value means it is dominated by a single group. Blocks with no population are shown grey.

Search any block name, GEOID, census tract, or county to jump to that location and open its full attribute record in the side panel. The current view, variable, and selection are encoded in the URL, so any view can be shared or bookmarked.
